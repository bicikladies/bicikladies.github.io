2026 Rapha Women’s 100 Srbija

Since 2013, the Rapha Women’s 100 ride has been held worldwide. It’s not just about cycling 100 kilometers, it’s about unity, support, and the joy of cycling.

It doesn’t matter if this is your first hundred kilometers or far from your first, this day is for every woman who wants to challenge herself and experience the unique spirit of a women’s ride. This isn’t just empty words — women’s rides always have a very special atmosphere ❤️

For BicikLadies, this will be our fifth Women’s 100 — in 2022, there were only three of us, but in 2025 there were 30+!

🚴‍♀️ What do you need to join the ride?

  • A road-ready bike. Please check your brakes, tires, and gears before the ride. To ensure a smooth and enjoyable 100 km ride for everyone, this event is designed for road and gravel bikes. Unfortunately, city and mountain bikes are not suitable for the distance and planned group pace.
  • Helmet. A helmet is mandatory, and you cannot participate without it — we won’t let you join the ride. Yes, we’re that strict because we prioritize road safety.
  • A basic repair kit. Strongly recommended! A spare inner tube, tire levers, a pump, and a multitool.
  • Water and snacks. We’ll stop at shops along the way, but please bring an initial supply of water and food just in case. Bananas, energy bars, and gummies are all great options. We don’t recommend chocolate-coated bars — they’re likely to melt.

🗓️ Timing

08:50 — Meet at Le Bol

09:20 — Briefing

09:30 — Start!

This year, we’re starting in Novi Sad! Participants travelling from Belgrade have several options:

  • Arrive on Saturday, stay overnight in Novi Sad and, if you wish, arrange shared accommodation together;
  • Come by car;
  • Take the train: there are departures at 7:30 and 8:00. Both arrive in Petrovaradin at approximately the same time, and from there you’ll need to cycle to the starting point.

To make the journey comfortable for everyone and avoid overcrowding the trains, join the chat! We can coordinate who is taking which train, who would like to share a car and who wants to arrange accommodation together in Novi Sad.

🧭 Route

🌿 Health Group — 105 km / 470 m elevation gain

A more relaxed option with predominantly gentle terrain. However, the route will not be completely flat: one noticeable, sustained climb awaits us. Please pace yourself accordingly — we have not only 100 kilometres ahead, but also a small test of endurance. We’ll ride at a comfortable pace and support one another 💛

⛰ Advanced Group — 118 km / 1,060 m elevation gain

A route for experienced and well-prepared riders. Fruška Gora, a long climb and well-earned descents await us. The terrain will require strength, endurance and confident bike-handling skills.
